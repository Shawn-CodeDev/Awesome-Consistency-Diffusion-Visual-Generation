# Additional literature batch 03 · papers 41–60

> Part of the 200-paper literature expansion for the survey repository.

## Normative consistency

- **[TINA: Text-Free Inversion Attack for Unlearned Text-to-Image Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Xiang_TINA_Text-Free_Inversion_Attack_for_Unlearned_Text-to-Image_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Evaluation study. Probes whether visual representations of erased concepts persist despite text-centric unlearning.
- **[Closed-Form Concept Erasure via Double Projections](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zhang_Closed-Form_Concept_Erasure_via_Double_Projections_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Uses two constrained closed-form projections to remove target concepts while retaining unrelated representations.
- **[GenErase: Generalizable and Semantically-Aware Concept Erasure in Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Vardhana_GenErase_Generalizable_and_Semantically-Aware_Concept_Erasure_in_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method + Benchmark. Uses preservation-aware geometric operations in attention value space; introduces GenBench-40 for erasure generalization.
## Internal consistency

- **[WorldReel: 4D Video Generation with Consistent Geometry and Motion Modeling](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Fang_WorldReel_4D_Video_Generation_with_Consistent_Geometry_and_Motion_Modeling_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Jointly generates RGB, pointmaps, camera trajectories and flow to tie video appearance to persistent 4D structure.
- **[STCDiT: Spatio-Temporally Consistent Diffusion Transformer for High-Quality Video Super-Resolution](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Chen_STCDiT_Spatio-Temporally_Consistent_Diffusion_Transformer_for_High-Quality_Video_Super-Resolution_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Uses motion-aware reconstruction and anchor-frame guidance to retain temporal and structural fidelity in generative video restoration.
## Normative consistency

- **[PhysGen: Physically Grounded 3D Shape Generation for Industrial Design](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/You_PhysGen_Physically_Grounded_3D_Shape_Generation_for_Industrial_Design_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Combines flow matching and physics-guided refinement in a joint shape-physics latent representation.
## External consistency

- **[You Only Erase Once: Erasing Anything without Bringing Unexpected Content](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zhu_You_Only_Erase_Once_Erasing_Anything_without_Bringing_Unexpected_Content_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. ## Normative consistency

- **[Bridging Privacy and Provenance: Traceable Virtual Identity Generation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zeng_Bridging_Privacy_and_Provenance_Traceable_Virtual_Identity_Generation_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Generates stable virtual identities with pose-preserving conditioning and latent provenance signals.
- **[Prototype-Guided Concept Erasure in Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Cai_Prototype-Guided_Concept_Erasure_in_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Clusters concept embeddings into prototypes for inference-time removal of broad undesired semantic categories.
- **[PhyCo: Learning Controllable Physical Priors for Generative Motion](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Narayanan_PhyCo_Learning_Controllable_Physical_Priors_for_Generative_Motion_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method + Dataset. Combines simulation data, physical-property conditioning and reward feedback to control physically plausible video dynamics.
## External consistency

- **[PoseAnything: General Pose-guided Video Generation with Part-aware Temporal Coherence](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wang_PoseAnything_General_Pose-guided_Video_Generation_with_Part-aware_Temporal_Coherence_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method + Dataset. Supports arbitrary skeletons using part-level temporal correspondence and decoupled subject-camera guidance.
## Internal consistency

- **[Long Video Diffusion Generation with Segmented Cross-Attention and Content-Rich Video Data Curation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Yan_Long_Video_Diffusion_Generation_with_Segmented_Cross-Attention_and_Content-Rich_Video_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method + Dataset. ## External consistency

- **[SwitchCraft: Training-Free Multi-Event Video Generation with Attention Controls](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Xu_SwitchCraft_Training-Free_Multi-Event_Video_Generation_with_Attention_Controls_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Steers frame-level attention toward corresponding event prompts to prevent event blending while retaining temporal coherence.
## Internal consistency

- **[CineScene: Implicit 3D as Effective Scene Representation for Cinematic Video Generation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Huang_CineScene_Implicit_3D_as_Effective_Scene_Representation_for_Cinematic_Video_Generation_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method + Dataset. Conditions video generation on implicit 3D scene features to preserve environments under camera motion and dynamic subjects.
- **[Identity-Preserving Image-to-Video Generation via Reward-Guided Optimization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Shen_Identity-Preserving_Image-to-Video_Generation_via_Reward-Guided_Optimization_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Uses reward-guided video diffusion optimization to preserve human identity under expression and motion changes.
- **[InstructMix2Mix: Consistent Sparse-View Editing Through Multi-View Model Personalization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Gilo_InstructMix2Mix_Consistent_Sparse-View_Editing_Through_Multi-View_Model_Personalization_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. - **[Dynamic-eDiTor: Training-Free Text-Driven 4D Scene Editing with Multimodal Diffusion Transformer](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Lee_Dynamic-eDiTor_Training-Free_Text-Driven_4D_Scene_Editing_with_Multimodal_Diffusion_Transformer_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Combines spatiotemporal attention and context-token propagation to edit 4D scenes without geometric or temporal drift.
- **[SMRABooth: Subject and Motion Representation Alignment for Customized Video Generation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Xu_SMRABooth_Subject_and_Motion_Representation_Alignment_for_Customized_Video_Generation_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Aligns object-level appearance and motion representations during customization to retain reference identity and motion.
