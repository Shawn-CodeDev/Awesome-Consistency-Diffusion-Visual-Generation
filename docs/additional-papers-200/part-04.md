## External consistency

- **[Nestwork: Conditional 3D Furnished House Layout Generation through Latent Heterogeneous Graph Diffusion](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Miao_Nestwork_Conditional_3D_Furnished_House_Layout_Generation_through_Latent_Heterogeneous_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. - **[M3DLayout: A Multi-Source Dataset of 3D Indoor Layouts and Structured Descriptions for 3D Generation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zhang_M3DLayout_A_Multi-Source_Dataset_of_3D_Indoor_Layouts_and_Structured_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Dataset + Benchmark. - **[Visual Prototype Conditioned Focal Region Generation for UAV-Based Object Detection](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Li_Visual_Prototype_Conditioned_Focal_Region_Generation_for_UAV-Based_Object_Detection_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Uses visual prototypes and focal-region synthesis to reduce missing, extra and misaligned objects in layout-guided generation.
