# Additional literature batch 05 · papers 81–100

> Part of the 200-paper literature expansion for the survey repository.

## Normative consistency

- **[Goal Force: Teaching Video Models To Accomplish Physics-Conditioned Goals](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Gillman_Goal_Force_Teaching_Video_Models_To_Accomplish_Physics-Conditioned_Goals_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method + Dataset. Conditions video generation on force goals and synthetic causal primitives to model action consequences.
- **[Improving Text-to-Image Generation with Intrinsic Self-Confidence Rewards](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Kim_Improving_Text-to-Image_Generation_with_Intrinsic_Self-Confidence_Rewards_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Derives intrinsic reinforcement-learning rewards from denoising self-confidence and combines them with external alignment signals.
- **[Detect-and-Guide: Self-regulation of Diffusion Models for Safe Text-to-Image Generation via Guideline Token Optimization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Li_Detect-and-Guide_Self-regulation_of_Diffusion_Models_for_Safe_Text-to-Image_Generation_via_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method. Detects unsafe content in noisy latents and applies localized adaptive guidance while preserving benign prompt content.
- **[JailbreakDiffBench: A Comprehensive Benchmark for Jailbreaking Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Jin_JailbreakDiffBench_A_Comprehensive_Benchmark_for_Jailbreaking_Diffusion_Models_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Benchmark. Standardizes evaluation of moderation and adversarial robustness across image and video diffusion systems.
- **[MapReduce LoRA: Advancing the Pareto Front in Multi-Preference Optimization for Generative Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Chen_MapReduce_LoRA_Advancing_the_Pareto_Front_in_Multi-Preference_Optimization_for_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Trains and combines reward-specific LoRA experts and token embeddings to reduce conflicts among visual-generation preferences.
- **[Designing Instance-Level Sampling Schedules via REINFORCE with James-Stein Shrinkage](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Yu_Designing_Instance-Level_Sampling_Schedules_via_REINFORCE_with_James-Stein_Shrinkage_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Learns prompt- and noise-conditioned sampling schedules to improve compositional and textual alignment without changing backbone weights.
- **[Multimodal RewardBench 2: Evaluating Omni Reward Models for Interleaved Text and Image](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Hu_Multimodal_RewardBench_2_Evaluating_Omni_Reward_Models_for_Interleaved_Text_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Benchmark. Evaluates reward models on expert preference pairs for image generation, editing and interleaved multimodal tasks.
- **[Test-Time Alignment of Text-to-Image Diffusion Models via Null-Text Embedding Optimisation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Kim_Test-Time_Alignment_of_Text-to-Image_Diffusion_Models_via_Null-Text_Embedding_Optimisation_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Optimizes unconditional guidance embeddings at test time to steer reward alignment in a semantic space.
## External consistency

- **[PromptEnhancer: Taming Your Rewriter for Text-to-Image Generation via Fine-Grained Reward](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wang_PromptEnhancer_Taming_Your_Rewriter_for_Text-to-Image_Generation_via_Fine-Grained_Reward_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Trains a structured prompt rewriter to improve attribute binding, negation and complex prompt interpretation.
## Normative consistency

- **[FairGen: Enhancing Fairness in Text-to-Image Diffusion Models via Self-Discovering Latent Directions](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Jiang_FairGen_Enhancing_Fairness_in_Text-to-Image_Diffusion_Models_via_Self-Discovering_Latent_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Learns attribute directions without a manually curated reference dataset for debiasing image generation.
- **[T2ISafety: Benchmark for Assessing Fairness, Toxicity, and Privacy in Image Generation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Li_T2ISafety_Benchmark_for_Assessing_Fairness_Toxicity_and_Privacy_in_Image_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Benchmark + Dataset + Evaluator. Provides annotated generations and an evaluator for fairness, harmful-content and privacy risks in image generators.
- **[Towards Fine-Grained Attribution: Instance-Aware Preference Optimization for Aligning Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Sun_Towards_Fine-Grained_Attribution_Instance-Aware_Preference_Optimization_for_Aligning_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Assigns preference credit to individual instances rather than propagating one global image preference to every region.
## Internal consistency

- **[MV-Adapter: Multi-View Consistent Image Generation Made Easy](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Huang_MV-Adapter_Multi-View_Consistent_Image_Generation_Made_Easy_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Adds geometric attention and unified camera-geometry conditioning to image generators for consistent multi-view synthesis.
- **[FlexGen: Flexible Multi-View Generation from Text and Image Inputs](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Xu_FlexGen_Flexible_Multi-View_Generation_from_Text_and_Image_Inputs_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Combines 3D-aware textual descriptions and image conditioning to control appearance while maintaining multi-view agreement.
- **[Bootstrap3D: Improving Multi-view Diffusion Model with Synthetic Data](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Sun_Bootstrap3D_Improving_Multi-view_Diffusion_Model_with_Synthetic_Data_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method + Dataset. Filters synthetic multi-view data and reschedules training timesteps to improve prompt fidelity and cross-view consistency.
- **[Reangle-A-Video: 4D Video Generation as Video-to-Video Translation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Jeong_Reangle-A-Video_4D_Video_Generation_as_Video-to-Video_Translation_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Learns view-invariant motion and consistent initial views to synchronize multi-view videos from one source video.
- **[SpinMeRound: Consistent Multi-View Identity Generation Using Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Galanakis_SpinMeRound_Consistent_Multi-View_Identity_Generation_Using_Diffusion_Models_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Combines multiple reference views with identity embeddings for consistent head generation across viewing directions.
- **[GAS: Generative Avatar Synthesis from a Single Image](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Lu_GAS_Generative_Avatar_Synthesis_from_a_Single_Image_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Conditions video diffusion on reconstructed geometry and appearance for temporally coherent multi-view avatars.
- **[MaterialMVP: Illumination-Invariant Material Generation via Multi-view PBR Diffusion](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/He_MaterialMVP_Illumination-Invariant_Material_Generation_via_Multi-view_PBR_Diffusion_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Regularizes multi-view material generation for lighting invariance and aligned albedo and roughness textures.
- **[WorldStereo: Bridging Camera-Guided Video Generation and Scene Reconstruction via 3D Geometric Memories](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zhang_WorldStereo_Bridging_Camera-Guided_Video_Generation_and_Scene_Reconstruction_via_3D_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Uses global and stereo geometric memories to preserve scene structure during camera-guided video generation.
