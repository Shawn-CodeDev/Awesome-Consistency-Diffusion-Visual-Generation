# Additional literature batch 02 · papers 21–40

> Part of the 200-paper literature expansion for the survey repository.

## External consistency

- **[BlobGEN-Vid: Compositional Text-to-Video Generation with Blob Video Representations](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Feng_BlobGEN-Vid_Compositional_Text-to-Video_Generation_with_Blob_Video_Representations_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method. Conditions video diffusion on blob trajectories and regional semantics to control motion and cross-frame object appearance.
- **[KV-Edit: Training-Free Image Editing for Precise Background Preservation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Zhu_KV-Edit_Training-Free_Image_Editing_for_Precise_Background_Preservation_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Preserves background DiT tokens through a key-value cache while regenerating only user-selected regions.
- **[GLASS: Guided Latent Slot Diffusion for Object-Centric Learning](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Singh_GLASS_Guided_Latent_Slot_Diffusion_for_Object-Centric_Learning_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method. Uses semantic and instance guidance to learn object slots supporting compositional generation of realistic multi-object scenes.
## Normative consistency

- **[TRCE: Towards Reliable Malicious Concept Erasure in Text-to-Image Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Chen_TRCE_Towards_Reliable_Malicious_Concept_Erasure_in_Text-to-Image_Diffusion_Models_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Targets implicitly expressed malicious concepts while retaining ordinary image-generation capabilities.
## External consistency

- **[AnyEdit: Mastering Unified High-Quality Image Editing for Any Idea](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Yu_AnyEdit_Mastering_Unified_High-Quality_Image_Editing_for_Any_Idea_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method + Dataset. Provides diverse instruction-edit pairs and task-aware diffusion routing for more reliable multi-type image editing.
## Normative consistency

- **[EraseAnything++: Enabling Concept Erasure in Rectified Flow Transformers Leveraging Multi-Object Optimization](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.00978)** — arXiv 2026 · Method. Balances erasure and utility in flow-transformer image and video models with spatial-temporal anchoring.
- **[Where Concept Erasure Should Occur: Concept-Layer Alignment in Text-to-Video Diffusion Models](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25941)** — arXiv 2026 · Method. Selects layers by concept separation to suppress target concepts while limiting degradation of video generation.
- **[Personalized Preference Fine-tuning of Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Dang_Personalized_Preference_Fine-tuning_of_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method. Conditions diffusion preference optimization on user embeddings rather than one population-level reward.
## Internal consistency

- **[ID-Patch: Robust ID Association for Group Photo Personalization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Zhang_ID-Patch_Robust_ID_Association_for_Group_Photo_Personalization_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method. Associates facial identities with spatial positions to reduce identity leakage in multi-person generation.
- **[SerialGen: Personalized Image Generation by First Standardization Then Personalization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Xie_SerialGen_Personalized_Image_Generation_by_First_Standardization_Then_Personalization_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method. Standardizes reference images before personalized synthesis to preserve whole-body appearance across prompts.
- **[Movie Weaver: Tuning-Free Multi-Concept Video Personalization with Anchored Prompts](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Liang_Movie_Weaver_Tuning-Free_Multi-Concept_Video_Personalization_with_Anchored_Prompts_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method. Uses anchored prompts to bind multiple reference identities and reduce identity blending in personalized video.
## External consistency

- **[HierEdit: Region-Aware Hierarchical Diffusion for Efficient High-Resolution Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zhang_HierEdit_Region-Aware_Hierarchical_Diffusion_for_Efficient_High-Resolution_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Refines edited regions under low-resolution structural guidance while reusing unmodified high-resolution content.
## Normative consistency

- **[Synthetic Video Enhances Physical Fidelity in Video Synthesis](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Zhao_Synthetic_Video_Enhances_Physical_Fidelity_in_Video_Synthesis_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method + Evaluation study. Transfers physically consistent cues from graphics-generated videos to video synthesis models.
## External consistency

- **[CARE-Edit: Condition-Aware Routing of Experts for Contextual Image Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wang_CARE-Edit_Condition-Aware_Routing_of_Experts_for_Contextual_Image_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Dynamically routes text, mask and reference conditions to specialized experts to reduce editing-condition conflicts.
- **[CogniEdit: Dense Gradient Flow Optimization for Fine-Grained Image Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Li_CogniEdit_Dense_Gradient_Flow_Optimization_for_Fine-Grained_Image_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Combines instruction decomposition and trajectory-level reward gradients for precise attribute editing.
- **[UniCombine: Unified Multi-Conditional Combination with Diffusion Transformer](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Wang_UniCombine_Unified_Multi-Conditional_Combination_with_Diffusion_Transformer_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method + Dataset. Combines subject, spatial and textual conditions in DiTs; provides SubjectSpatial200K for multi-condition alignment.
## Normative consistency

- **[GrOCE : Graph-Guided Online Concept Erasure for Text-to-Image Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Han_GrOCE__Graph-Guided_Online_Concept_Erasure_for_Text-to-Image_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Builds dynamic semantic graphs for context-aware suppression of target prompt concepts while retaining other semantics.
- **[Erasing Thousands of Concepts: Towards Scalable and Practical Concept Erasure for Text-to-Image Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Seo_Erasing_Thousands_of_Concepts_Towards_Scalable_and_Practical_Concept_Erasure_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Uses concept-distribution modeling and mixture-of-experts erasure to scale removal while preserving neighboring embeddings.
- **[Neighbor-Aware Localized Concept Erasure in Text-to-Image Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Shi_Neighbor-Aware_Localized_Concept_Erasure_in_Text-to-Image_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Combines neighbor-aware embedding modulation and spatial gating to erase targets without suppressing related concepts.
- **[EMMA: Concept Erasure Benchmark with Comprehensive Semantic Metrics and Diverse Categories](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wei_EMMA_Concept_Erasure_Benchmark_with_Comprehensive_Semantic_Metrics_and_Diverse_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Benchmark. Tests concept removal under implicit prompts, similar non-target concepts and demographic-bias diagnostics.
