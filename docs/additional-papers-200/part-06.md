# Additional literature batch 06 · papers 101–120

> Part of the 200-paper literature expansion for the survey repository.

## External consistency

- **[TrajectoryCrafter: Redirecting Camera Trajectory for Monocular Videos via Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Yu_TrajectoryCrafter_Redirecting_Camera_Trajectory_for_Monocular_Videos_via_Diffusion_Models_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Separates view transformation from generation and conditions on rendered geometry and source video for camera redirection.
## Internal consistency

- **[VideoRFSplat: Direct Scene-Level Text-to-3D Gaussian Splatting Generation with Flexible Pose and Multi-View Joint Modeling](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Go_VideoRFSplat_Direct_Scene-Level_Text-to-3D_Gaussian_Splatting_Generation_with_Flexible_Pose_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Jointly generates camera poses and multi-view images through separate streams with asynchronous denoising.
- **[OneStory: Coherent Multi-Shot Video Generation with Adaptive Memory](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/An_OneStory_Coherent_Multi-Shot_Video_Generation_with_Adaptive_Memory_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method + Dataset. Selects prior-shot frames and compresses context adaptively for identity- and narrative-consistent next-shot generation.
- **[SKALD: Learning-Based Shot Assembly for Coherent Multi-Shot Video Creation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Lu_SKALD_Learning-Based_Shot_Assembly_for_Coherent_Multi-Shot_Video_Creation_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Evaluator + Comparative method. Learns clip-assembly scores for temporal and semantic coherence when selecting candidate shots into narratives.
- **[PERSONA: Personalized Whole-Body 3D Avatar with Pose-Driven Deformations from a Single Image](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Sim_PERSONA_Personalized_Whole-Body_3D_Avatar_with_Pose-Driven_Deformations_from_a_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Balances diffusion-generated poses and source-image constraints to reduce identity shifts in personalized 3D avatars.
## External consistency

- **[UniVBench: Towards Unified Evaluation for Video Foundation Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wei_UniVBench_Towards_Unified_Evaluation_for_Video_Foundation_Models_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Benchmark + Evaluator. Evaluates instruction-based multi-shot generation and editing with human-created videos and unified agentic scoring.
## Internal consistency

- **[PersonaHOI: Effortlessly Improving Face Personalization in Human-Object Interaction Generation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Hu_PersonaHOI_Effortlessly_Improving_Face_Personalization_in_Human-Object_Interaction_Generation_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method. Fuses general and personalized diffusion priors for identity-consistent human-object interaction images.
- **[CubeComposer: Spatio-Temporal Autoregressive 4K 360deg Video Generation from Perspective Video](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Li_CubeComposer_Spatio-Temporal_Autoregressive_4K_360deg_Video_Generation_from_Perspective_Video_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Generates cubemap faces in a spatiotemporal autoregressive order to extend perspective videos into panoramic sequences.
- **[ShotDirector: Directorially Controllable Multi-Shot Video Generation with Cinematographic Transitions](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wu_ShotDirector_Directorially_Controllable_Multi-Shot_Video_Generation_with_Cinematographic_Transitions_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Combines parameterized camera control and editing-pattern prompts for intentional transitions in coherent multi-shot narratives.
- **[STAGE: Storyboard-Anchored Generation for Cinematic Multi-shot Narrative](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zhang_STAGE_Storyboard-Anchored_Generation_for_Cinematic_Multi-shot_Narrative_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method + Dataset. Uses start-end storyboard pairs and multi-shot memory to retain entities and cinematic continuity; introduces ConStoryBoard.
- **[DreamShot: Personalized Storyboard Synthesis with Video Diffusion Prior](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Huang_DreamShot_Personalized_Storyboard_Synthesis_with_Video_Diffusion_Prior_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Uses video priors and role-attention consistency for personalized multi-character storyboard generation.
## External consistency

- **[I2I-Bench: A Comprehensive Benchmark Suite for Image-to-Image Editing Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wang_I2I-Bench_A_Comprehensive_Benchmark_Suite_for_Image-to-Image_Editing_Models_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Benchmark + Evaluator. Evaluates single- and multi-image editing with task-specific tools and decoupled instruction-following and preservation criteria.
- **[3D Space as a Scratchpad for Editable Text-to-Image Generation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Saha_3D_Space_as_a_Scratchpad_for_Editable_Text-to-Image_Generation_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Uses editable 3D scene assets and rendered cues as a spatial interface for controlling downstream image generation.
- **[VINS-120K: Ultra High-Resolution Image Editing with A Large-Scale Dataset](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Chen_VINS-120K_Ultra_High-Resolution_Image_Editing_with_A_Large-Scale_Dataset_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Dataset + Benchmark + Method. Provides high-resolution instruction-edit pairs and evaluation resources for studying edit fidelity and high-frequency detail preservation.
- **[UniGlyph: Unified Segmentation-Conditioned Diffusion for Precise Visual Text Synthesis](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Wang_UniGlyph_Unified_Segmentation-Conditioned_Diffusion_for_Precise_Visual_Text_Synthesis_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method + Benchmark. Combines segmentation masks, glyph conditioning, and region-aware supervision to improve visual-text accuracy.
- **[TextMaster: A Unified Framework for Realistic Text Editing via Glyph-Style Dual-Control](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Yan_TextMaster_A_Unified_Framework_for_Realistic_Text_Editing_via_Glyph-Style_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Separates glyph and style control to edit textual content while maintaining typography, appearance, and layout.
- **[Omni IIE Bench: Benchmarking the Practical Capabilities of Image Editing Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Yang_Omni_IIE_Bench_Benchmarking_the_Practical_Capabilities_of_Image_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Benchmark. Tests shared-context single-turn edits and multi-turn editing to diagnose instruction adherence and accumulated degradation.
- **[Circuit Mechanisms for Spatial Relation Generation in Diffusion Transformers](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wang_Circuit_Mechanisms_for_Spatial_Relation_Generation_in_Diffusion_Transformers_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Analysis. Analyzes internal circuits for object attributes and spatial relations in diffusion transformers and their generalization behavior.
- **[FonTS: Text Rendering With Typography and Style Controls](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Shi_FonTS_Text_Rendering_With_Typography_and_Style_Controls_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method + Dataset. Introduces word-level typography controls and a style-conditioning mechanism for controllable visual-text generation.
- **[MotionEdit: Benchmarking and Learning Motion-Centric Image Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wan_MotionEdit_Benchmarking_and_Learning_Motion-Centric_Image_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method + Benchmark. Studies motion-directed image edits with a dedicated benchmark and motion-flow-based optimization signals.
