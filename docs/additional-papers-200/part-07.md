# Additional literature batch 07 · papers 121–140

> Part of the 200-paper literature expansion for the survey repository.

## External consistency

- **[Exploring Spatial Intelligence from a Generative Perspective](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Zhu_Exploring_Spatial_Intelligence_from_a_Generative_Perspective_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Benchmark + Dataset. Evaluates spatial manipulation and visual fidelity in generative editing using real and synthetic spatial tasks.
- **[Inter-Edit: First Benchmark for Interactive Instruction-Based Image Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Liu_Inter-Edit_First_Benchmark_for_Interactive_Instruction-Based_Image_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Benchmark + Dataset. Assesses localized interactive image edits with position-aware metrics and explicitly specified interaction patterns.
- **[FlowDC: Flow-Based Decoupling-Decay for Complex Image Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Jiang_FlowDC_Flow-Based_Decoupling-Decay_for_Complex_Image_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method + Benchmark. Decouples complex edits into parallel sub-edits and suppresses destructive velocity components to preserve source structure.
- **[IGD: Instructional Graphic Design with Multimodal Layer Generation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Qu_IGD_Instructional_Graphic_Design_with_Multimodal_Layer_Generation_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Combines multimodal layer planning, diffusion asset generation, and parametric rendering for instruction-controlled graphic design.
- **[FlashIn: Fast and Accurate Image Inversion for Real-time Image Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Wang_FlashIn_Fast_and_Accurate_Image_Inversion_for_Real-time_Image_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Learns cycle-consistent image-to-noise inversion to improve reconstruction fidelity and preserve image content during editing.
- **[BiFM: Bidirectional Flow Matching for Few-Step Image Editing and Generation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Dai_BiFM_Bidirectional_Flow_Matching_for_Few-Step_Image_Editing_and_Generation_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Jointly models image-to-noise and noise-to-image transport with bidirectional consistency for controllable few-step edits.
- **[Inpaint4Drag: Repurposing Inpainting Models for Drag-Based Image Editing via Bidirectional Warping](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Lu_Inpaint4Drag_Repurposing_Inpainting_Models_for_Drag-Based_Image_Editing_via_Bidirectional_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Converts drag instructions into bidirectional pixel warping and inpainting while preserving natural object deformation.
- **[ChordEdit: One-Step Low-Energy Transport for Image Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Lu_ChordEdit_One-Step_Low-Energy_Transport_for_Image_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Constructs a low-energy source-to-target editing field to reduce object distortion and changes outside edited regions.
- **[DragText: Rethinking Text Embedding in Point-Based Image Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2025/html/Choi_DragText_Rethinking_Text_Embedding_in_Point-Based_Image_Editing_WACV_2025_paper.html)** — WACV 2025 · Method. Updates and regularizes text embeddings alongside point-based dragging to maintain alignment with the evolving image.
- **[Optimal Transport for Rectified Flow Image Editing: Unifying Inversion-Based and Direct Methods](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/html/Lupascu_Optimal_Transport_for_Rectified_Flow_Image_Editing_Unifying_Inversion-Based_and_WACV_2026_paper.html)** — WACV 2026 · Method. Uses transport-guided inversion and velocity corrections to balance edit flexibility, structural preservation, and reconstruction fidelity.
## Internal consistency

- **[DreamLayer: Simultaneous Multi-Layer Generation via Diffusion Model](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Huang_DreamLayer_Simultaneous_Multi-Layer_Generation_via_Diffusion_Model_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method + Dataset. Models foreground-background interactions and shared attention to preserve occlusion, layout, and appearance across generated layers.
## External consistency

- **[Language-Free Generative Editing from One Visual Example](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Elezabi_Language-Free_Generative_Editing_from_One_Visual_Example_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Extracts a visual transformation condition from a paired example and corrects inversion errors to preserve source details.
- **[Interpretable Prompts made Edit-Friendly: Token-to-Token Similarity Reduction in dLLMs for Edit-Friendly Hard Prompt Inversion](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Devulapally_Interpretable_Prompts_made_Edit-Friendly_Token-to-Token_Similarity_Reduction_in_dLLMs_for_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Derives reference-aligned interpretable prompts whose token edits retain controllability in downstream image generators.
- **[Vinedresser3D: Towards Agentic Text-guided 3D Editing](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Chi_Vinedresser3D_Towards_Agentic_Text-guided_3D_Editing_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Decomposes editing instructions and applies rectified-flow inpainting in native 3D latents while preserving unedited geometry.
- **[GeoDiffusion: A Training-Free Framework for Accurate 3D Geometric Conditioning in Image Generation](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Mueller_GeoDiffusion_A_Training-Free_Framework_for_Accurate_3D_Geometric_Conditioning_in_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Uses a parametric 3D prior and geometry-guided dragging to control 2D image geometry and reference viewpoint.
- **[UniEdit-I: Training-free Image Editing for Unified VLM via Iterative Understanding, Editing and Verifying](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Bai_UniEdit-I_Training-free_Image_Editing_for_Unified_VLM_via_Iterative_Understanding_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Uses a closed-loop understanding-editing-verification process to adapt semantic edits to intermediate alignment feedback.
- **[DICE: Discrete Inversion Enabling Controllable Editing for Masked Generative Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/WACV2026/html/He_DICE_Discrete_Inversion_Enabling_Controllable_Editing_for_Masked_Generative_Models_WACV_2026_paper.html)** — WACV 2026 · Method. Records discrete reverse-process noise and masks to support image reconstruction and controlled edits in discrete diffusion models.
## Normative consistency

- **[RewardFlow: Generate Images by Optimizing What You Reward](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Susladkar_RewardFlow_Generate_Images_by_Optimizing_What_You_Reward_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Combines differentiable rewards and an adaptive inference policy for semantic alignment and localized edit preservation.
- **[PromptLoop: Plug-and-Play Prompt Refinement via Latent Feedback for Diffusion Model Alignment](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Lee_PromptLoop_Plug-and-Play_Prompt_Refinement_via_Latent_Feedback_for_Diffusion_Model_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Trains a prompt-refinement policy that observes intermediate latents to improve reward alignment and limit over-optimization.
- **[Reward Sharpness-Aware Fine-Tuning for Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Kim_Reward_Sharpness-Aware_Fine-Tuning_for_Diffusion_Models_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Robustifies reward gradients through parameter and sample perturbations to reduce reward hacking during diffusion fine-tuning.
