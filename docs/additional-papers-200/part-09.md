# Additional literature batch 09 · papers 161–180

> Part of the 200-paper literature expansion for the survey repository.

## External consistency

- **[FlowEdit: Inversion-Free Text-Based Editing Using Pre-Trained Flow Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Kulikov_FlowEdit_Inversion-Free_Text-Based_Editing_Using_Pre-Trained_Flow_Models_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Directly transports between source- and target-prompt distributions to edit images without noise inversion.
- **[FiVE-Bench: A Fine-grained Video Editing Benchmark for Evaluating Emerging Diffusion and Rectified Flow Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Li_FiVE-Bench_A_Fine-grained_Video_Editing_Benchmark_for_Evaluating_Emerging_Diffusion_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Benchmark + Method. Evaluates object-level edit success, background preservation, and temporal consistency for diffusion and rectified-flow video editors.
## Internal consistency

- **[Gloria: Consistent Character Video Generation via Content Anchors](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Yang_Gloria_Consistent_Character_Video_Generation_via_Content_Anchors_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Uses compact character-centric anchor frames to stabilize appearance and identity across generated video views and time.
- **[DriveScape: High-Resolution Driving Video Generation by Multi-View Feature Fusion](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Wu_DriveScape_High-Resolution_Driving_Video_Generation_by_Multi-View_Feature_Fusion_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method. Fuses multi-view features with 3D road and object conditions for controlled, mutually compatible driving videos.
- **[Geometry-as-context: Modulating Explicit 3D in Scene-consistent Video Generation to Geometry Context](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2026/html/Hu_Geometry-as-context_Modulating_Explicit_3D_in_Scene-consistent_Video_Generation_to_Geometry_CVPR_2026_paper.html)** — CVPR 2026 · Method. Alternates geometry estimation and novel-view restoration inside camera-controlled autoregressive scene generation.
- **[StreamingT2V: Consistent, Dynamic, and Extendable Long Video Generation from Text](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/html/Henschel_StreamingT2V_Consistent_Dynamic_and_Extendable_Long_Video_Generation_from_Text_CVPR_2025_paper.html)** — CVPR 2025 · Method. Combines short-term conditional attention, long-term appearance memory, and chunk blending for coherent extended video generation.

## Internal consistency

- **[UniPortrait: A Unified Framework for Identity-Preserving Single- and Multi-Human Image Personalization](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/He_UniPortrait_A_Unified_Framework_for_Identity-Preserving_Single-_and_Multi-Human_Image_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. - **[Steering Guidance for Personalized Text-to-Image Diffusion Models](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Park_Steering_Guidance_for_Personalized_Text-to-Image_Diffusion_Models_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. - **[PersonaCraft: Personalized and Controllable Full-Body Multi-Human Scene Generation Using Occlusion-Aware 3D-Conditioned Diffusion](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/ICCV2025/html/Kim_PersonaCraft_Personalized_and_Controllable_Full-Body_Multi-Human_Scene_Generation_Using_Occlusion-Aware_ICCV_2025_paper.html)** — ICCV 2025 · Method. Uses 3D body geometry and occlusion-aware conditioning to preserve full-body identity in multi-person scenes.
