# Taxonomy

This survey organizes diffusion-based visual generation around three consistency relations.

## External consistency

**Agreement target:** generated outputs should satisfy conditions specified outside the sample, including prompts, layouts, poses, masks, references, and editing instructions.

Representative subproblems:

- prompt and compositional consistency
- grounded and structural consistency
- edit consistency

## Internal consistency

**Agreement target:** generated states should remain mutually compatible across subjects, views, frames, or narrative states.

Representative subproblems:

- subject and identity consistency
- multi-view and 3D consistency
- temporal and narrative consistency

## Normative consistency

**Agreement target:** outputs should satisfy evaluative criteria that are not fully specified by the prompt.

Representative subproblems:

- preference and aesthetic consistency
- safety and value consistency
- physical, commonsense, and causal consistency

## Auxiliary axes

Each method or resource can also be described by:

1. observation unit
2. agreement target
3. optimization locus
4. evidence source
5. trade-off profile
